About Us

From burned corn cobs to a home air-care innovation

EcoCorn Solutions was founded in 2025 in Chiang Mai, Thailand, by a team combining expertise in product design, environmental science, and business management.

Every year, PM 2.5 pollution makes people sick with coughing, sneezing, and flare-ups of allergies, forcing them to stay indoors with the AC running constantly. At the same time, one major cause of this toxic air is the burning of leftover corn cobs from agriculture. We saw a way to solve both problems at once.

Our Mission

Protect people's health from PM 2.5 while reducing agricultural waste

EcoCorn Filter is a simple, accessible, eco-friendly, and healthy choice for every household.

Problem & Solution

A pollution cycle solved by waste material

The Problem

Burning leftover corn cobs from farms creates PM 2.5 pollution, making people sick and forcing them to stay shut indoors with the AC running non-stop.

→

EcoCorn's Solution

We turn corn cobs into carbon filters that trap dust and odors in the air, while creating extra income for farmers from what used to be waste.
